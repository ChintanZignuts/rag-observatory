# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are managers who need a clear view of HR policy assistant quality and the developer maintaining the RAG pipeline. They use the dashboard to inspect evaluation health, compare runs, test questions, and decide whether the assistant is reliable enough for internal use.

## Product Purpose

This product is an AI quality engineering dashboard for an HR policy RAG assistant. It exists to make retrieval quality, generated answers, evaluation results, and future observability signals visible in one place. Success means managers can understand quality trends quickly, and the developer can drill into failed answers and retrieved context without leaving the workflow.

## Brand Personality

Serious, precise, operational. The interface should feel like an enterprise observability tool: restrained, data-forward, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style landing pages, decorative SaaS cards, playful chatbot aesthetics, oversized hero sections, and bright consumer-product styling. The dashboard should not look like a generic AI demo or a presentation site.

## Design Principles

- Lead with operational signal: surface counts, scores, run status, and failures before explanation.
- Make drill-down fast: every summary should have a path to the underlying run, question, answer, or source.
- Keep the visual system restrained: use color mainly for state, priority, and selection.
- Treat uncertainty honestly: missing scores, pending integrations, and empty states should be explicit.
- Support repeated use: layouts should be dense enough for scanning without becoming cramped.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Use readable contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and chart/table layouts that do not rely on color alone.
